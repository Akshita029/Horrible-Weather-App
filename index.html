HORRIBLE WEATHER APP
a small coding project
My weather app project

Weather, but less convenient.

I started with a normal weather app and then decided to experiment with bad UX without breaking the actual weather feature.

Six sliders are used to enter the city name, and the weather button is further down the page.

Watch it work

This is the app running. The video shows how a city is entered, how the interface is navigated, and what happens when the weather is finally requested.

The demo is about 40 seconds long.

What I changed

The main idea was simple: keep the weather lookup working, but make some normal interactions unnecessarily inconvenient.

01

Entering a city

I used six vertical sliders instead of giving the user a normal text-entry experience.

02

Finding the button

The weather button is lower down in the scroll area, so it takes a little more effort to reach.

03

Showing the result

The app displays the real weather along with sarcastic comments and emojis.

The app still does its job. I just made getting there unnecessarily complicated.

What I was trying to learn

This project was mainly an experiment with PyQt5, layouts, widgets, scrolling, and making a working interface behave in an unusual way.

PYQT5

Widgets and layouts

I worked with labels, sliders, buttons, layouts, and a scroll area to build the interface.

UX

Designing friction

I wanted the interface to look reasonably clean while making the interactions less convenient.

EXPERIMENT

Still functional

The point was not to break the app. The weather lookup still needs to work.
